## Exercise demonstrations

The start/finish photos in public/exercises/ (shown on /training) come from
[free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released under the
[Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE.md) (public domain dedication).
The photos were resized and converted to WebP. The dataset does not document who originally took
the photos, so their provenance can't be verified; replace them if a rights holder raises a concern
or before relying on them commercially. The catalog is in src/lib/training/exercise-catalog.ts.
